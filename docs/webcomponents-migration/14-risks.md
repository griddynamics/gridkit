# 14 — Risks and Mitigations

**Owner:** CTORNDSD-646d · **Answers:** CTORNDSD-646 acceptance criterion 17 · **Status:** Delivered

Every one of `FINDINGS.md`'s 5 GO conditions and 10 carried-forward open questions is accounted for
below — either closed with evidence, or restated as a live risk with a mitigation and an owner. Nothing
from the spike is silently dropped.

## Unrelated findings surfaced along the way

Not migration risks, but real and worth someone's attention:

- **`@react-spring/web` appears to be an unused dependency** — zero usages anywhere in `libs/`, not in
  `knip.json`'s ignore list, yet shipped as a runtime dependency so consumers install it for nothing.
- **`npm run storybook:test` has 2 pre-existing failures** in `ChatContainer.stories.play.ts`, proven
  unrelated to this work by reverting and reproducing them identically. Needs its own ticket.
- **`libs/ui`'s `CLAUDE.md` is out of date** on the export surface — it documents four subpaths; the
  package declares seven.
